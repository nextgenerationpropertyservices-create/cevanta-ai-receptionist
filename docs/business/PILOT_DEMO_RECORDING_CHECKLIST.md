# Pilot demo recording checklist

Date: 2026-10-06
Use: record a safe one-time demo video for HVAC prospects.

## Before recording

- Use fictional data only.
- Close provider dashboards unless they are needed and safe to show.
- Do not show Retell, Make, Twilio, Supabase, Vercel, Gmail, Google Calendar, or payment settings if any private data is visible.
- Do not show webhook URLs, API keys, tokens, account IDs, transcripts, recordings, real phone numbers, real customer records, payment details, or private screenshots.
- Use the production app at `https://cevanta-ai-receptionist.vercel.app/` only if signed in safely and showing fictional demo content.
- Keep the video to 5–8 minutes.

## Pages to show

Show these pages in order if available:

1. Dashboard / workspace overview
2. Setup / Onboarding
3. Leads
4. Jobs
5. Calendar
6. Launch
7. Integrations
8. Pilot

## Required talking points

- Cevanta is a managed pilot.
- Office review stays in control.
- The offer is `$497 setup + $297/month`.
- No automatic booking yet.
- No SMS/email/calendar writes yet.
- Retell phone answering has proof with limitations.
- Make safe intake has proof with limitations.
- Production Cevanta writer stays off until reviewed and approved.

## Fictional demo data

Use names like:

- Northstar HVAC Demo
- Test Customer
- 123 Fictional Street
- AC repair / no cooling
- Tomorrow afternoon
- office@example.invalid
- +15550000001

## Retell section

Say:

> Retell has answered a live test call, but for the pilot we still keep office review in place. The AI should collect details and avoid confirming appointments.

Do not show transcripts, recordings, caller numbers, or private Retell identifiers.

## Make/Cevanta section

Say:

> Make safe intake and duplicate handling have been tested with fictional data. The Cevanta bridge is verifier-only until production lead writing is approved.

Do not show Make webhook URLs, scenario secrets, payloads with private data, or provider credentials.

## Stop rules to mention

- Pause if AI promises a confirmed appointment.
- Pause if Make creates duplicates.
- Pause if Retell credits are low.
- Pause before SMS/email/calendar writes.
- Pause before billing or paid tools.

## After recording

Before sending the video:

- Watch it once all the way through.
- Confirm no secrets or private data are visible.
- Confirm you did not promise automatic booking.
- Confirm the price is stated correctly.
- Confirm the call to action is a 10-minute fit call.

## Demo link message

Use this when sending the video:

> Here’s the quick Cevanta demo I mentioned. It shows the managed HVAC AI receptionist pilot: calls become office-review service requests, and your team stays in control of appointments and customer messages.
>
> [paste demo link]
>
