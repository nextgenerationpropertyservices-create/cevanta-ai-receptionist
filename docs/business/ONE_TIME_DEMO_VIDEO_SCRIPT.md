# One-time Cevanta demo video script

Date: 2026-10-06
Length: 5–8 minutes
Audience: HVAC owners or office managers who are hiring or need receptionist/dispatch help
Offer: `$497 setup + $297/month` managed pilot

Use only fictional data. Do not show provider dashboards, webhook URLs, API keys, real phone numbers, real customer records, recordings, transcripts, payment data, or private screenshots.

## Demo title

Cevanta HVAC AI Receptionist Pilot — 7 Minute Demo

## Core promise

Say this early:

> Cevanta is a managed AI receptionist pilot for HVAC businesses. It answers calls, captures service requests, and puts them into an office review flow. During the pilot, your office confirms appointments and customer messages before anything is finalized.

## Screen 1 — Opening problem, 30 seconds

Show: production app sign-in page or already signed-in dashboard using fictional data.

Say:

> If your HVAC business is hiring for reception, dispatch, or office help, the problem is usually not just answering the phone. It is capturing the right details, keeping requests organized, and making sure your office follows up. Cevanta is built to help with that.

> This first version is a managed pilot. It is not full automatic booking. The AI captures the request, and your office stays in control.

## Screen 2 — Dashboard overview, 60 seconds

Show: workspace overview/dashboard.

Say:

> This is the Cevanta workspace. Each business has its own dashboard. The office can see leads, jobs, calendar, setup, launch readiness, integrations, and the pilot runbook from one place.

Point out:

- workspace navigation
- leads
- jobs
- calendar
- setup
- launch
- integrations
- pilot

Say:

> The goal is simple: turn calls into organized office-review requests so fewer leads are missed.

## Screen 3 — Setup, services and hours, 60–90 seconds

Show: Setup / Onboarding page.

Say:

> Before live calls, we set up the business profile, services, hours, request preferences, and escalation contacts. This tells the pilot what the office needs to collect and what the AI should never promise.

Show if available:

- business profile
- services
- weekly hours
- date exceptions
- request preferences
- escalation contacts

Say:

> For the pilot, the AI can collect preferred time, urgency, service address, and customer details. The office still confirms availability.

## Screen 4 — Leads and AI request review, 90 seconds

Show: Leads page with fictional AI lead/request.

Say:

> When an AI receptionist call creates a request, the office reviews it here. New AI-created leads are highlighted so they do not get buried.

Point out:

- lead name or fictional request
- status/priority
- AI receptionist source if visible
- office review flow

Say:

> This is where the pilot stays safe. The AI captures the request. A person reviews it before a confirmed appointment or customer message goes out.

## Screen 5 — Jobs and internal calendar, 60–90 seconds

Show: Jobs page, then Calendar page.

Say:

> After review, the office can move work toward jobs and scheduling. Cevanta has a job and internal calendar foundation, but during the pilot, we do not claim automatic Google Calendar writes or automatic booking unless that is separately approved and tested.

Point out:

- job board
- internal calendar
- fictional appointment

Say:

> This lets the owner see how the workflow will grow without turning on risky automation too early.

## Screen 6 — Launch and Integrations pages, 60–90 seconds

Show: Launch page, then Integrations page.

Say:

> These pages keep the pilot honest. They separate what is ready from what still needs approval.

Say:

> Retell phone answering has passed a live inbound test with limitations. Make safe intake has passed fictional duplicate-handling tests. The Cevanta bridge is verifier-only until production writing is reviewed and approved.

Point out:

- Retell status
- Make status
- Cevanta lead intake status
- Twilio/SMS off
- Calendar/billing off if shown

Say:

> SMS, email, external calendar writes, billing, and full automatic booking are not part of the first pilot.

## Screen 7 — Pilot page / operating runbook, 45 seconds

Show: Pilot page if available.

Say:

> The pilot runbook gives the office a simple operating rhythm: review requests, confirm outcomes manually, watch urgent calls, and pause if anything looks wrong.

Say:

> The stop rules are clear: pause if the AI promises a booking, if Make creates duplicates, if Retell credits are low, or before any SMS, email, or calendar writes are enabled.

## Screen 8 — Offer and call to action, 45 seconds

Show: dashboard or Launch/Pilot page.

Say:

> The first managed pilot is $497 setup plus $297 per month. That includes basic setup, call-capture workflow, and office-review support for one business/location.

> If this looks useful, reply and I will walk you through a quick 10-minute fit call. We will collect your services, hours, call rules, and escalation contacts, then run a safe dry run before turning on any live workflow.

## Claims to avoid

Do not say:

- fully automated booking is live
- SMS/email/calendar automation is ready
- Cevanta replaces office staff
- emergency dispatch is guaranteed
- every Retell/Make/live provider path is fully proven
- this is complete self-service SaaS
- customers can be messaged automatically today
