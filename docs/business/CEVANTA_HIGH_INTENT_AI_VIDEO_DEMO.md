## Full 6–8 minute voiceover script and storyboard

Target length: about 7 minutes.

### Scene 1 — Hook: missed calls cost money

Time: 0:00–0:40
Screen: Title slide or demo dashboard hero area.
On-screen text: “Missed HVAC calls become missed revenue.”

Voiceover:

If you own an HVAC company, you already know how expensive missed calls can be. A customer calls because their furnace is making a grinding noise, their AC is blowing warm air, or they need a tune-up before the season changes. If nobody answers, they usually do not wait. They call the next company.

Cevanta is built for that exact problem. It is an AI receptionist pilot for HVAC businesses that answers calls, captures the service request, and organizes the information so your office can review and follow up faster.

This first version is designed to be safe and practical. It does not replace your office. It helps your office stop losing the calls that should have become leads.

### Scene 2 — What Cevanta does

Time: 0:40–1:20
Screen: Show demo dashboard top section.
On-screen text: “AI call intake → office-review lead → faster follow-up.”

Voiceover:

Here is the simple version. When a call comes in, the AI receptionist answers professionally, asks the right intake questions, and captures the details your office needs: name, callback number, service issue, location, urgency, and preferred appointment window.

Then Cevanta turns that call into an office-review lead. Your team can look at the request, decide what to do next, and follow up with the customer.

During the managed pilot, the AI does not confirm appointments by itself. It does not send customer texts or emails by itself. It does not create calendar events, collect payment, quote jobs, or dispatch technicians unless those features are separately approved, tested, and turned on later.

The goal of the pilot is simple: answer more calls, capture better service requests, and give the office a clean review process.

### Scene 3 — Show fictional demo dashboard

Time: 1:20–2:05
Screen: Open https://cevanta-ai-receptionist.vercel.app/demo-dashboard and slowly scroll the top section.
On-screen text: “Fictional demo data only.”

Voiceover:

This is a fictional demo dashboard, so none of the names, addresses, or jobs are real. It is here to show how the system works without exposing private customer information.

At the top, you can see the managed-pilot boundary. This demo does not send SMS, email, payment, quote, dispatch, or confirmed appointment actions. That is intentional. For the first pilot, the business stays in control.

The dashboard gives the owner or office team a simple way to understand what came in, what needs review, and what should happen next.

### Scene 4 — Show AI receptionist leads

Time: 2:05–3:05
Screen: Scroll to “AI receptionist leads.” Highlight fictional examples: Sam Carter, Pine Street Market, Casey Morgan.
On-screen text: “Every call becomes a clear service request.”

Voiceover:

Here is what a captured call can look like. In this example, Sam Carter called about a furnace making a grinding noise. The dashboard shows the service issue, the location, the urgency, and the preferred window.

For an HVAC office, that is the difference between a missed call and a useful lead. The office does not have to guess what the customer wanted. The request is organized in one place.

Here is another example: Pine Street Market has AC blowing warm air and would like service today if possible. That kind of request may need faster review. Cevanta helps surface that information so the office can decide whether to call back, schedule, or mark it for follow-up.

And here is a maintenance request. Not every call is urgent, but it can still become revenue if it is captured and followed up properly.

### Scene 5 — Explain office-review workflow

Time: 3:05–3:55
Screen: Show demo talk track card or lead list.
On-screen text: “Office review first. Confirmation second.”

Voiceover:

The key part of this pilot is office review. Cevanta captures the request, but your office keeps control.

That means the AI can ask for a preferred appointment window, but it should not tell the customer the appointment is confirmed. The office reviews availability first. Then a real person, or a later approved automation, can confirm the appointment.

This matters because HVAC scheduling can be messy. Jobs run long. Emergency calls come in. Certain customers need different handling. Service areas can change by day. Cevanta is built to help your office move faster without making promises your team cannot keep.

### Scene 6 — Jobs and calendar preview

Time: 3:55–4:45
Screen: Scroll to “Jobs and calendar preview.” Show scheduled hold, new review item, completed demo item.
On-screen text: “Organized follow-up without unsafe automation.”

Voiceover:

The next section shows jobs and calendar-style review items. Again, this is fictional data, but it shows the workflow.

A request can become a scheduled hold, a new review item, or a completed record. The office can see what needs attention and what has already been handled.

For the first managed pilot, this is the right level of control. It proves that calls can be captured and organized before the business turns on more advanced automation like confirmed calendar booking or customer messaging.

### Scene 7 — Setup snapshot and onboarding

Time: 4:45–5:35
Screen: Show setup snapshot section. If useful, briefly show production dashboard sign-in or onboarding page, without private data.
On-screen text: “Services, hours, call rules, escalation.”

Voiceover:

Cevanta also needs to know how the business works. That is handled during onboarding.

The client gives their service area, services offered, normal hours, closed days, call handling rules, and escalation contacts. The most important part is the “never promise” rules. For example, the AI should not promise confirmed appointments, exact pricing, dispatch, or emergency response unless the business has approved that exact behavior.

This is what makes the pilot safer. We are not just turning on a generic bot. We are setting up a managed receptionist flow around how the HVAC office actually operates.

### Scene 8 — Retell AI phone demo

Time: 5:35–6:20
Screen: Show Retell phone demo panel or a clean call demo screen. Do not show billing, keys, webhooks, or private settings.
On-screen text: “AI answers. Office reviews.”

Voiceover:

The voice side is powered by a live AI phone agent. For a demo call, the AI can answer like a receptionist, ask one question at a time, collect the service details, and close by explaining that the office will review and follow up.

A good call should feel simple. The AI should collect the caller’s name, phone number, service issue, location, urgency, and preferred time. If the caller gives a phone number, it should confirm the number digit by digit. If the area code is missing, it should ask for it.

The important part is what it does not say. It should not say the appointment is booked. It should not promise dispatch. It should not quote a price. It should capture the request and move it into office review.

### Scene 9 — Managed pilot offer

Time: 6:20–7:10
Screen: Return to demo dashboard closing section or show a simple offer slide.
On-screen text: “Managed pilot: $497 setup + $297/month.”

Voiceover:

The first offer is a managed pilot. Setup is $497, then $297 per month.

That includes setting up the AI receptionist flow, collecting the business rules, preparing the office-review process, running test calls, and helping the business prove whether this can save missed opportunities.

This is best for HVAC owners who are missing calls, stretched thin in the office, or thinking about hiring a receptionist but want to test a lower-cost system first.

The pilot is intentionally simple. Answer calls. Capture service requests. Create office-review leads. Help the team follow up faster.

Time: 7:10–7:45
Screen: Show demo dashboard hero or final offer slide.
On-screen text: “Book a 10-minute fit call.”

Voiceover:

If you own an HVAC company and you are losing calls because the office is busy, Cevanta may be a fit.

The next step is a quick 10-minute fit call. We will look at your call flow, your service area, your office-review process, and whether a managed AI receptionist pilot makes sense for your business.

Cevanta is not here to overpromise. It is here to help you answer more calls, capture better service requests, and give your office a cleaner way to follow up.

Book a 10-minute fit call, and we will see if the pilot is right for you.
