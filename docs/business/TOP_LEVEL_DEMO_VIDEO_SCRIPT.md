# Top-level Cevanta demo video script

Date: 2026-10-06
Length: 2–3 minutes
Use: send to HVAC owners before a live call
Audience: owners, managers, and office leads who are hiring or struggling with call coverage

This is the short marketing demo. It should explain the problem, the pilot offer, the office-review boundary, and the next step. It does not need to show every screen.

## Video title

Cevanta AI Receptionist for HVAC — Managed Pilot Overview

## Goal

Get the prospect to say yes to a 10-minute fit call.

## Tone

Simple, direct, owner-friendly. Do not sound like software jargon.

## Script

### 0:00–0:20 — Hook

Show: Cevanta dashboard or simple title slide.

Say:

> If you run an HVAC company and you are trying to hire reception or dispatch help, Cevanta is built for the problem you are already feeling: missed calls, messy details, and service requests that do not get followed up fast enough.

### 0:20–0:45 — What Cevanta does

Show: dashboard overview or Leads page.

Say:

> Cevanta is an AI receptionist pilot for HVAC businesses. It helps answer calls, capture the service request, and organize the details so your office can review the next step.

> The goal is not to replace your office. The goal is to catch more calls and make follow-up easier.

### 0:45–1:15 — Pilot boundary

Show: Leads page or Launch page.

Say:

> The first pilot is office-review first. That means the AI can collect the caller's name, phone number, service address, issue, urgency, and preferred time. Your office still confirms appointments and customer messages before anything is finalized.

> We do not start with risky automatic booking, SMS, email, or calendar changes.

### 1:15–1:45 — What the owner gets

Show: Setup, Leads, Jobs, Calendar quickly, one screen each.

Say:

> In the pilot, we set up your services, hours, call rules, and escalation contacts. Calls become organized requests in the dashboard. Your team can review leads, move work toward jobs, and keep the schedule organized.

> Everything starts small and controlled, with one business or location.

### 1:45–2:10 — Proof and current state

Show: Launch or Integrations page.

Say:

> Cevanta already has the dashboard, setup flow, leads, jobs, calendar foundation, Retell voice answering proof, and Make safe-intake proof. The live pilot keeps automation controlled so your office stays in charge.

### 2:10–2:35 — Offer

Show: simple offer slide or dashboard.

Say:

> The first managed pilot is $497 setup plus $297 per month. That includes basic setup, the call-capture workflow, and support during the pilot.

### 2:35–3:00 — Call to action

Show: Cevanta dashboard or title slide.

Say:

> If this looks useful, reply and I will show you the workflow in a quick 10-minute fit call. We will see if Cevanta can help cover call overflow while you are hiring or improving your office process.

## Claims to avoid

Do not say:

- fully automatic booking is live
- SMS/email/calendar automation is live
- Cevanta replaces a receptionist
- emergency dispatch is guaranteed
- every integration is fully automated
- no human review is needed

## Screens to show

Keep this simple. Use 4–6 quick visuals:

1. Dashboard overview
2. Setup/services/hours
3. Leads or AI request review
4. Jobs/calendar foundation
5. Launch/Integrations readiness
6. Offer/CTA slide if you make one

## Safe recording checklist

Before sending, confirm the video shows no:

- real customer names
- real customer phone numbers or addresses
- Retell transcripts or recordings
- Make webhook URLs
- API keys or secrets
- payment details
- provider account IDs
- private dashboards with sensitive details
