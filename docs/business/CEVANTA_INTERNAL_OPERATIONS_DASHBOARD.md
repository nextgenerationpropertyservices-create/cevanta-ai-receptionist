# Cevanta Internal Operations Dashboard

Updated: October 10, 2026

Production workspace:
https://cevanta-ai-receptionist.vercel.app/workspaces/1cb2a226-84ef-4dcd-9976-5ce87dd3e449

Workspace name:
Cevanta Internal Operations

Purpose:
This is the internal Cevanta operations workspace. It is for Cevanta testing, demo practice, pilot monitoring, internal leads and operating review. It is not a permanent client workspace.

Client workspace rule:
Each HVAC client should have their own workspace. Cevanta operator access should be retained as owner/admin or support admin so onboarding, leads, jobs, setup, and support can be reviewed.

Where client setup appears:
When a client fills out setup in their dashboard, review it in that client's workspace under `Setup`. The basic business profile is also visible under `Settings`.

Internal weekly use:
- Check Leads for AI receptionist test/pilot leads.
- Check Jobs and Calendar for office-review flow.
- Check Setup for internal operating rules.
- Check Launch and Integrations before enabling live client flow.
- Keep client-specific records in each client's own workspace and client folder.
