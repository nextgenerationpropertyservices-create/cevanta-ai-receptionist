# CEV-PILOT-SALES-69D — First-client managed-pilot sales readiness packet

Status: accepted with limitations as documentation
Owner: Morgan — Product Manager and Orchestrator
Date: 2026-10-05

## Scope

Create an owner-facing sales readiness packet for the first managed-pilot client. The packet should combine the accepted intake, demo, local walkthrough and Make/Retell dry-run package into a simple sellable first-version guide: what to offer, who is a good first client, what to say, what not to promise, what evidence to collect, and what approvals are required before live automation.

## Out of scope

- Pricing commitments, legal terms, service contracts, payment processing, production deployment or live-provider activation
- Changes to Make, Retell, Twilio, Google Calendar, SMS, email, Supabase or hosting accounts
- Claims that the app is fully production-ready or fully automated
- Use of real customer data, credentials, webhook URLs, provider secrets or private account screenshots

## Acceptance criteria

1. The packet states the first sellable offer in plain language as a managed pilot.
2. The packet identifies ideal and poor-fit first clients.
3. The packet gives a sales call structure, demo flow, discovery questions and close/follow-up steps.
4. The packet lists exact promises allowed and claims to avoid.
5. The packet defines evidence needed before moving from sales demo to live pilot.
6. Project status, backlog and next-task context are updated.

## Evidence

- Manual documentation inspection completed.
- No code, runtime, browser, provider, hosted database or production checks were run because this is a documentation-only sales readiness task.

## Acceptance decision

Accepted with limitations as documentation. The packet helps sell a managed first version honestly, but it does not prove live provider behavior, hosted behavior, production readiness, legal compliance, pricing acceptance, payment processing, or first-client delivery.
